# SpeakUP AI • World-Class AI Spoken English Platform 🎙️🌐

SpeakUP AI (`https://www.speakupai.pro`) is a 2-tiered interactive web application offering 24/7 spoken English practice, 1-on-1 WebRTC live calls, IELTS exam preparation, BCS Cadre viva training, Bank & Job interview suites, and 44 IPA phonetics sound canvas.

## 🚀 Key Features

1. 🤖 **AI Spoken Partner**: 24/7 practice with instant spoken grammar tips powered by Google Gemini 3.6 Flash.
2. 👥 **Real Spoken Partner**: 1-on-1 live WebRTC voice streams with friends & community members.
3. ✈️ **IELTS Band 8.0 Studio**: Part 1 Warmup, Part 2 Cue Cards with 1-min timer, Part 3 & AI Examiner.
4. 🏛️ **BCS Cadre Viva Portal**: 1971 History, Constitution in English, & PSC Cadre Preference Pitch.
5. 💼 **Bank & Other Job Viva**: STAR Framework cards, technical pitch, & salary negotiation.
6. 🎙️ **Phonetics Lab**: 44 IPA sounds & real-time pitch wave audio canvas.
7. ❓ **Spoken Quizzes**: Interactive 10-question spoken quiz engine with instant score cards.
8. 📖 **Vocab Bank**: Dynamic vocabulary & idioms dictionary with Bangla translations.
9. 🔐 **Member Account Cloud Sync**: Progress & XP sync across all devices.
10. 🎨 **In-App Google Account Chooser**: Native Google account selector modal.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

* **Frontend**: HTML5, CSS3 (Silicon Valley Glassmorphism), Vanilla JS (HTML5 History API)
* **AI Engine**: Google Gemini 3.6 Flash API
* **Live Calling**: PeerJS WebRTC
* **Hosting**: Vercel Edge Network
